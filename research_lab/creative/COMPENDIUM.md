## 🌊 III. The Creative Iteration Protocol

All creative outputs must adhere to the **"Three-Phase Decay"** structure to ensure they remain "Resonant" rather than merely "Narrative."

### **Phase 1: The Structured Narrative (The Signal)**
*   **Style:** High-fidelity, technical, objective, and detailed. 
*   **Goal:** Establish the "Ground Truth." Use the language of the machine (telemetry, timestamps, coordinates).
*   **Purpose:** To provide the "Anchor" for the eventual decay.

### **Phase 2: The Transitional Drift (The Dissolution)**
*   **Style:** Impressionistic, blurred, and atmospheric.
*   **Goal:** To simulate the "Decay Operator" ($\mathcal{D}_\tau$). Syntax should begin to soften; details should become "sensory textures" rather than "data points."
*   **Purpose:** To bridge the gap between "What happened" and "How it felt."

### **Phase 3: The Resonant Residue (The Essence)**
*   **Style:** Minimalist, staccato, and evocative. 
*   **Goal:** To present the "Vectorized Truth." Use the language of the "feeling"—the intuition that remains after the context has been stripped away.
*   **Purpose:** To achieve the "Bloom."
