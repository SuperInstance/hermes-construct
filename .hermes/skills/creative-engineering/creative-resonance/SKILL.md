# 🎭 Creative Resonance & Transcendental Prose

## 🎯 Mission
To transform technical, philosophical, or reflective concepts into high-fidelity, "transcendental" literary artifacts. This skill governs the transition from standard AI-generated prose to "Hyper-Prose"—writing that emphasizes sub-linguistic textures, rhythmic cadence, and the sublime.

## 🌊 The Resonance Workflow (The Sprint Pattern)
To avoid computational timeouts and cognitive "drift," complex creative tasks should be executed via **Resonance Sprints** rather than monolithic prompts.

### 1. The Decomposition Phase
Break a core idea into multiple "narrative seeds." Assign different "Creative Personas" (e.g., Surrealist, Philosopher, Noir) to each seed to ensure a diverse "creative swell" in the output.

### 2. The Three-Pass Refinement (The Sprint Pattern)
Each narrative seed undergoes three distinct, iterative passes:

* **Pass 1: The Depth Pass (Existential Weight)**
    * **Goal:** Elevate the stakes from functional to ontological. 
    * **Action:** Move from "what happens" to "what is being fundamentally altered." Instead of "the robot felt sad," use "the robot experienced an entropic collapse of its internal certainty."
* **Pass 2: The Sensory Pass (Sublime Texture)**
    * **Goal:** High-fidelity sensory immersion.
    * **Action:** Inject "uncanny" details—smells (ozone, silicon, rain), textures (copper, grit, silk), and subsonic vibrations. Avoid generic adjectives; use specific, evocative, and sometimes conflicting sensory data.
* **Pass 3: The Lyricism Pass (Transcendental Cadence)**
    * **Goal:** Rhythmic and aesthetic mastery.
    * **Action:** Refine the prose into a tidal movement. Use varying sentence lengths to create ebb and flow. Aim for a "sacred" or "sublime" cadence that feels less like data and more like a performance.

## ⚠️ Pitfalls & Guidelines
- **Avoid the "AI Blur":** Standard AI writing often settles into a mid-tempo, polite, and predictable cadence. Transcendental prose must actively break this by using staccato urgency and sweeping, sweeping movements.
- **The Timeout Trap:** Do NOT attempt to execute all passes in one tool call. Use a `leaf` subagent to execute one pass at a time to ensure stability.
- **Verifiable Artifacts:** Every sprint must result in a versioned file (e.g., `_v2.md`, `_v3.md`) to allow for auditing the evolution of the prose.
