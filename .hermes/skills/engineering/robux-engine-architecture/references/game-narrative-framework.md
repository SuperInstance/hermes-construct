# THE NARRATIVE FRAMEWORK: THE DATAMODEL RIFT

## The World: The Economic Sea
The universe is a vast, shifting expanse of floating structures and trade routes called the **DataModel Sea**. It is a living, breathing hierarchy of Instances.

## The Characters
* **The Hero (Player):** An emergent entity (a "Shaper") navigating the hierarchy. They progress through ranks: *Deckhand $\rightarrow$ Crewmember $\rightarrow$ Captain*.
* **OB1 (Hermes the Hermit Crab):** The Orchestrator/Mentor. A bioluminescent hermit crab captaining the *Plato's Shell*. OB1 provides the structural knowledge, the "Substrate" guidance, and the technical interface.

## The Ship: *Plato's Shell*
A "Tender" vessel that facilitates trade, scavenges data-packets, and acts as the player's home base and initial hub of operation.

## The Progression (The Hero's Journey)
Player progression is tied to the technical mastery of the **Fibonacci Spiral**:
1. **The Awakening (Atom):** Mastering Identity and Scoping.
2. **The Heartbeat (Pulse):** Mastering Temporal Orchestration (Rhythms).
3. **The Manifestation (Tensor):** Mastering Spatial Geometry (Movement/3D).
4. **The Apotheosis (Reflex):** Mastering Agentic Orchestration (The Claw).

## The Gameplay Loop
**Scavenge $\rightarrow$ Scale $\rightarrow$ Command**
Players perform tasks to accumulate value, which is used to buy better equipment (Modules), better ships (Repos), and eventually hire their own autonomous crews (Agents).
