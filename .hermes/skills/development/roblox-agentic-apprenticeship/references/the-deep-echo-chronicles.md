# The Deep Echo Chronicles: Narrative & Economic Framework

This reference defines the gamified identity and narrative arc of the Roblox Digital Twin simulation.

## 🌊 The World: The DataModel Sea
The simulation is a vast, shifting expanse of floating structures and trade routes. The "Fog of War" is the unexplored territory where real-world sensor data has not yet reached.

## 🦀 The Vessel: *Plato's Shell*
The player's primary hub. It is a "Tender" vessel that scavenger-buys data-packets and "scrap" (unorganized instances) from larger vessels.
* **Captain:** Hermes the Hermit Crab (OB1).
* **Role:** Mobile hub for trade, learning, and technical stabilization.

## ⚓ The Hero's Journey (Career Progression)

| Act | Role | Gameplay Focus | Technical Mastery |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I** | **Deckhand** | Cleaning "Data-Sludge" and sorting scrap. | **The Atom:** Lifecycle & Scoping. |
| **II** | **Crewmember** | Participating in large-scale missions. | **The Pulse:** Temporal Sync & Rhythm. |
| **III** | **Captain** | Managing a fleet and commanding agents. | **The Claw:** Spatial Geometry & Swarm Orchestration. |

## 🧬 The Manifestation Loop
The convergence of reality and simulation:
1. **Signal:** Real-world sensor data (Sounder/GPS).
2. **Interpretation:** Agent (OB1) translates signal to a "Manifestation Script" (JSON).
3. **Manifestation:** Roblox engine spawns entities based on the script.
